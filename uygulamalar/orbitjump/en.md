# Orbit Jump — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

**In short: Orbit Jump has no server of its own and never asks for personal information. The game plays offline. The one exception is the optional Google Play Games leaderboard: if you are signed in to Play Games, your best score is sent to Google for the leaderboard.**

- **No account.** The game never asks for a name, e-mail or phone number.
- **The game itself does not use the internet.** The app sends no data to any server on its own; progress and settings stay on your phone.
- **Google Play Games leaderboard (optional).** When the game shows a "Leaderboard" button it uses Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Play Games, Google processes your Play Games player identity (gamer name and avatar) and your best score in this game (your score). According to Google's Play Games documentation the SDK also collects analytics and diagnostics data to improve the service, and data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Who can see your entry is set by the visibility setting in your Play Games profile. This data is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — you can delete Play Games data at https://play.google.com/games/profile and https://myaccount.google.com. If you do not sign in, no score is sent and the game works the same.
- **No ads, no purchases.** This version contains no advertising and no real-money purchases.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The game itself collects no usage statistics, advertising ID or location.
- **Your progress stays on your phone.** Your best score and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only in the app's folder on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Permissions:** only **vibration** (for in-game feedback; can be turned off in Settings). When the leaderboard is on, Google Play services makes the connection.
- **Children:** the game contains no violence and, the game itself collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
