# Sibercinin Günlüğü — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 September 2026

---

## In short

**Sibercinin Günlüğü collects no personal data.**

The app runs entirely on your phone. There is no account to create, no
internet connection required, and no server the app could send anything
to — no such server was ever built.

---

## What data is collected

**None.** Not your name, e-mail, phone number, location, device
identifier, or usage statistics.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting
service, and no social media integration.

---

## What is stored on your phone

The app keeps two things in your phone's own storage:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Terms you bookmark | So you can find the words you starred again |
| Guides you mark as read | So you can see where you left off |

Neither ever leaves your phone. It is not sent to us, to Google, or to
anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

---

## Internet connection

The app does **not** need the internet to work. The whole dictionary, the
guides and the security bulletins are stored inside the app, on your
phone.

Android grants every app a default "internet" permission; seeing it in
the permission list does not mean the app connects to anything.
Sibercinin Günlüğü makes no network requests.

---

## Permissions requested

The app asks you for **no special permissions.** Camera, microphone,
location, contacts, files/storage, notifications — none are used and none
are requested.

Storage and "draw over other apps" permissions that some libraries add on
their own have been **deliberately blocked**, because the app does not
use them.

---

## Children

The app is aimed at adults working or interested in cyber security. No
data is knowingly collected from children — no data is collected from
anyone.

---

## Content and sources

The guides are compiled from open sources (OWASP Top 10, OWASP Mobile
Top 10 and similar). Sources are listed individually under
**Settings → Sources** in the app.

Tool names such as Kali Linux, Nmap and Burp Suite are trademarks of
their respective owners; the app is not affiliated with these projects
and describes them for educational purposes only.

---

## If this policy changes

If a feature that sends data is ever added (a live news feed, or an AI
question-and-answer module, for example), this policy will be rewritten
**in the same update** and will state plainly what is sent. A published
app whose behaviour does not match its policy is grounds for removal from
the store — and it is dishonest.
