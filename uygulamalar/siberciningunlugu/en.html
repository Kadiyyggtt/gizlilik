Sibercinin Günlüğü — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

In short

Sibercinin Günlüğü collects no personal data.

The app runs entirely on your phone. There is no account to create, no internet connection required, and no server the app could send anything to — no such server was ever built.

What data is collected

None. Not your name, e-mail, phone number, location, device identifier, or usage statistics.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, and no social media integration.

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps two things in your phone's own storage:

WhatWhy
Terms you bookmarkSo you can find the words you starred again
Guides you mark as readSo you can see where you left off

Neither ever leaves your phone. It is not sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

The technical log that stays on your device

If the app closes unexpectedly, a short technical log of what happened is kept on your phone only. It is never sent anywhere and never reaches any server.

  • You can read it yourself under Settings → Technical log.
  • You can delete it there with a single tap.
  • E-mail addresses, the user name inside file paths and long digit sequences are stripped out as it is written.
  • At most 10 entries are kept; the oldest is dropped when a new one arrives.

There is no automatic way to send it to us. If you want to, you can copy the text and send it yourself — the choice is entirely yours.

The log is not collected; it simply sits on your device.

Internet connection

The app does not need the internet to work. The whole dictionary, the guides and the security bulletins are stored inside the app, on your phone.

Android grants every app a default "internet" permission; seeing it in the permission list does not mean the app connects to anything. Sibercinin Günlüğü makes no network requests.

Permissions requested

The app asks you for no special permissions. Camera, microphone, location, contacts, files/storage, notifications — none are used and none are requested.

Storage and "draw over other apps" permissions that some libraries add on their own have been deliberately blocked, because the app does not use them.

Children

The app is aimed at adults working or interested in cyber security. No data is knowingly collected from children — no data is collected from anyone.

Content and sources

The guides are compiled from open sources (OWASP Top 10, OWASP Mobile Top 10 and similar). Sources are listed individually under Settings → Sources in the app.

Tool names such as Kali Linux, Nmap and Burp Suite are trademarks of their respective owners; the app is not affiliated with these projects and describes them for educational purposes only.

If this policy changes

If a feature that sends data is ever added (a live news feed, or an AI question-and-answer module, for example), this policy will be rewritten in the same update and will state plainly what is sent. A published app whose behaviour does not match its policy is grounds for removal from the store — and it is dishonest.
