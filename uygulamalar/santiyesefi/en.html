Site Boss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 Developer: Kadir Yiğit Contact: yyggttkadir@gmail.com

In short

Site Boss collects nothing.

No ads. No purchases. No accounts. No servers. The game works fully without an internet connection.

The only action that leaves the device is sharing, and it sits behind a parental gate and is optional: if the child wants to send a picture of their town to family, it is sent through the phone's own share sheet.

In this version all 18 buildings (the main town and the Big City) are free and unlocked.

What we do NOT collect

This app does not collect, store or share any of the following:

  • Name, email, phone number or any other personal information
  • Location
  • Photos, camera or microphone recordings
  • Contacts, calendar or other files on the device
  • Advertising identifiers or any other tracking identifiers
  • Usage statistics, analytics data, or crash reports sent to us (the technical log described below never leaves your device)

The technical log that stays on your device

If Şantiye Şefi closes unexpectedly, a short technical log of what happened is kept on your phone only. It is never sent anywhere and never reaches any server.

  • You can read it yourself under Settings → Technical Log.
  • You can delete it there with a single tap.
  • E-mail addresses, the user name inside file paths and long digit sequences are stripped out as it is written.
  • At most 10 entries are kept; the oldest is dropped when a new one arrives.

There is no automatic way to send it to us. If you want to, you can copy the text and send it yourself — the choice is entirely yours.

This is why the "we never collect" list above stays accurate: the log is not collected, it simply sits on your device.

Sharing

The game has a share button. When it is pressed:

  1. A parental gate appears first — a two-digit multiplication question. A child is not expected to pass it on their own; this step is required by Google Play's Families policy.
  2. If the question is answered correctly, the game renders a picture of the town and opens the phone's own share sheet.
  3. Where the picture goes (WhatsApp, email, saving to the gallery…) is entirely the user's choice.

Important points:

  • The picture never reaches a server of ours — we have no server.
  • The picture contains only the in-game town drawing: no personal information, no name, no photo of the child.
  • The game works completely without ever using sharing.
  • Once the picture is sent, what happens to it is governed by the privacy policy of the app that was chosen.

What is stored on the device

The game stores the following only on your own device:

  • The buildings you have finished and the state of your town
  • The progress of an unfinished job
  • The pictures in the album (explained separately below)
  • Your sound, vibration and language preferences

None of this ever leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase it at any time from Reset the town in the in-game Settings screen.

The album — pictures that stay on the device

When a building is finished, the game takes a picture of it and puts it in the app's own album. That picture:

  • Is the game's own drawing. The app has NO access to the camera, the photo gallery or any other picture on the phone, and asks for none.
  • Stays on this device only, in the app's own folder. It is never uploaded and nobody else can see it.
  • The album holds at most 24 pictures; when it is full the oldest one is deleted automatically, so a child's phone does not fill up.
  • Every picture can be deleted individually from the album.
  • Deleting the app deletes every picture with it.

Sending a picture out of the album counts as sharing: the parental gate opens first, then the phone's own share sheet.

Permissions the app declares on Android:

Microphone, camera, location, contacts, storage and store billing (BILLING) permissions are deliberately blocked — the app has no need for them.

Children

This game is designed for children and built to follow the rules of the Google Play Families programme.

  • No ads — neither personalised nor otherwise.
  • No in-app purchases in this version. All 18 buildings are free and none of them is locked.
  • The only action that leaves the app is sharing, which sits behind a parental gate and is optional.
  • No chat, messaging or interaction with other users.
  • Children are never asked for any information, and none is collected.

Because no data is collected at all, there is no processing that would require parental consent under COPPA or GDPR-K.

Third parties

The app contains no ad networks, analytics tools or payment services. Site Boss shares no data with anyone, because it collects none.

Changes

If ads or purchases are ever added, this policy will be updated first and the change announced on the app's store page. (29 September 2026: this version has no purchases; every building, the Big City included, is free.)
