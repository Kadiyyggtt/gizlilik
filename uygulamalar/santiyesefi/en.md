# Site Boss — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 11 September 2026
**Developer:** Kadir Yiğit
**Contact:** yyggttkadir@gmail.com

---

## In short

**Site Boss collects nothing.**

No ads. No accounts. No servers. The game itself never connects to the
internet.

The one exception is **sharing**: if the child wants to send a picture of
their town to family, that picture is sent through the phone's own share
sheet. That step sits behind a **parental gate** and is entirely optional.

---

## What we do NOT collect

This app does not collect, store or share any of the following:

- Name, email, phone number or any other personal information
- Location
- Photos, camera or microphone recordings
- Contacts, calendar or other files on the device
- Advertising identifiers or any other tracking identifiers
- Usage statistics, crash reports or analytics data

---

## Sharing

The game has a **share** button. When it is pressed:

1. A **parental gate** appears first — a two-digit multiplication question.
   A child is not expected to pass it on their own; this step is required
   by Google Play's Families policy.
2. If the question is answered correctly, the game renders a **picture of
   the town** and opens the phone's **own share sheet**.
3. Where the picture goes (WhatsApp, email, saving to the gallery…) is
   **entirely the user's choice**.

Important points:

- The picture never reaches a server of ours — we have no server.
- The picture contains only the in-game town drawing: no personal
  information, no name, no photo of the child.
- The game works completely **without ever using sharing**.
- Once the picture is sent, what happens to it is governed by the privacy
  policy of the app that was chosen.

---

## What is stored on the device

The game stores the following **only on your own device**:

- The buildings you have finished and the state of your town
- The progress of an unfinished job
- **The pictures in the album** (explained separately below)
- Your sound, vibration and language preferences

None of this ever leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
You can also erase it at any time from **Reset the town** in the in-game
Settings screen.

---

## The album — pictures that stay on the device

When a building is finished, the game takes a **picture** of it and puts
it in the app's own album. That picture:

- Is **the game's own drawing**. The app has NO access to the camera,
  the photo gallery or any other picture on the phone, and asks for none.
- Stays **on this device only**, in the app's own folder. It is never
  uploaded and nobody else can see it.
- The album holds **at most 24 pictures**; when it is full the oldest one
  is deleted automatically, so a child's phone does not fill up.
- Every picture can be **deleted individually** from the album.
- Deleting the app deletes every picture with it.

Sending a picture out of the album counts as **sharing**: the parental
gate opens first, then the phone's own share sheet.

## Permissions

Permissions the app declares on Android:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `VIBRATE` | Light vibration when digging and placing panels |
| `INTERNET` | Added by the application framework; the game does **not** connect to the internet |

Microphone, camera, location, contacts and storage permissions are
**deliberately blocked** — the app has no need for them.

---

## Children

This game is designed for children and built to follow the rules of the
Google Play **Families** programme.

- No ads — neither personalised nor otherwise.
- No in-app purchases.
- The only action that leaves the app is **sharing**, which sits behind a
  parental gate and is optional.
- No chat, messaging or interaction with other users.
- Children are never asked for any information, and none is collected.

Because no data is collected at all, there is no processing that would
require parental consent under COPPA or GDPR-K.

---

## Third parties

The app contains **no third-party services, ad networks or analytics
tools.** No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.
