HisseRadar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

HisseRadar is an educational app that teaches stock market strategies and lists, every trading day, the BIST and US stocks that match those strategies.

Data we collect

The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. Your lesson progress, quiz results, watchlist and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to a server. Deleting the app deletes them.

Internet use

On launch and when you tap "Refresh", the app downloads the day's scan result (a JSON file) from a public address. No device identifier is sent with that request. The result is the same for everyone.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications for a scan, your device subscribes to that topic and receives a notification when a new match appears. Subscriptions are topic based; no personal record is kept. You can turn them off on the same screen.

Ads and purchases

This version contains no ads and no purchases. If they are added, this policy will be updated and announced inside the app.

Children

The app is intended for adults; no data is knowingly collected from anyone under 18. No personal data is collected at all.

Not investment advice

Lessons and scan results are for education only and are not investment advice. Trading decisions and their outcomes belong to the user.
