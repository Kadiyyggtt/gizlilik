# Saha11 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Saha11 helps five-a-side football groups find a missing player or goalkeeper. A captain posts a request, nearby players apply, and accepted players meet in a temporary group chat. The app is developed by Kadir Yiğit, who is the data controller.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile phone number | Creating an account and signing in (SMS code) | Only on the authentication server; never shown to other players |
| Nickname, position | So other players can recognise you | Public profile |
| Location (only while the app is open) | Listing nearby requests, being visible to captains when you say "available", confirming you arrived at the pitch | On the server; other players see your distance rounded to half a kilometre, never your exact location |
| Match records | Applications, acceptances, pitch check-ins, post-match ratings | On the server |
| Group messages | Pre-match coordination between accepted players | On the server; the room becomes read-only 2 hours after the match ends |
| Notification token | "An urgent request opened near you" notifications | On the server; deleted when you turn notifications off |

We do not ask for your name, email, photo, contacts or payment details.

## A clear word on location
- Location is read only while you are using the app. There is no background tracking and the app never requests that permission.
- When you switch the "availability beacon" off, your stored location is deleted from the server.
- Arrival verification (150 metres) is computed on the server; only "at the pitch" or "not" is kept.

## Ratings and reliability
A player who is accepted and does not show up, or who withdraws less than 3 hours before kick-off, loses reliability points. After a match, other players give a fair-play rating. These appear on your profile; you can contest them at the address below.

## Who your data is shared with
- With other players: nickname, position, ratings and approximate distance. Never your phone number or exact location.
- With service providers: data is processed by Supabase (database and authentication, EU servers) and the SMS provider used for sign-in codes. Notifications are delivered through Expo.
- Never sold or shared for advertising. The app has no ads and no tracking.

## Retention and deletion
You can delete your account from Settings or by writing to the address below. Profile, location, messages and notification records are deleted within 30 days; only match records that affect other players' ratings are kept in anonymised form.

## Children
The app is for people aged 18 and over. Age confirmation is required at sign-in; no data is knowingly collected from anyone under 18.

## Your rights
Under GDPR and Turkish data protection law (KVKK) you may access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. Requests are answered within 30 days.

## Reporting and blocking
You can block and report any player. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours; accounts may be closed.

## Changes
If this policy changes, this page is updated and the change is announced inside the app.
