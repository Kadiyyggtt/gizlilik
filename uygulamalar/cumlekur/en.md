# CümleKur — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

CümleKur is an English-learning app made for Turkish speakers. This
document states plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

**Short answer:** There is no account, we never ask who you are, there
are no ads and no tracking. All of your learning progress stays **on
your own device**. The app does not send data to the internet on its own.
The one thing to know about is the optional "Say it out loud" feature
(shown in Turkish as "Sesli söyle"): if the app cannot confirm that your
voice will stay on the phone, it asks you first, and the phone's system
service processes the audio **only if you agree** (see the "Microphone"
section below).

---

## What we never collect

The app does not request, collect or transmit:

- Your real name, e-mail address or phone number (you may type an
  optional name on the Profile screen; it stays only on your device and
  in your phone's own backup, see below)
- Location
- Contacts, calendar, photos or files
- Camera. The microphone is used only **when you tap the "Say" button**
  (shown in Turkish as "Söyle"); audio is **not recorded, stored, or sent
  to us** (separate section below)
- Advertising identifier (the app contains no ads)
- Usage analytics, or crash reports **sent to us**
  (the technical log described below never reaches us)

We have **no account system.** You do not sign in to the app.

---

## The technical log that stays on your device

If CümleKur closes unexpectedly, or an error is caught and the app keeps
running, a short technical log of what happened is kept
**on your phone only**. The app never sends it anywhere; because it sits in
the same folder as your progress record, it is included only in your phone's
**own backup** (below). We cannot see or receive it.

- You can read it yourself under **Settings → Technical Log**.
- You can delete it there with a single tap.
- E-mail addresses, the user name inside file paths and long digit
  sequences are stripped out as it is written.
- At most 10 entries are kept; the oldest is dropped when a new one arrives.

There is no automatic way to send it to us. If you want to, you can copy
the text and send it yourself — the choice is entirely yours.

This is why the "we never collect" list above stays accurate: the log is
**not collected**, it simply sits on your device.

## What is stored on your device

The following is kept **only on your own device**:

- The name you type on the Profile screen (optional; it does not have to
  be your real name, and "Öğrenci" (Student) is shown if you leave it
  blank). If you choose to share your progress, this name is part of the
  text you share.
- Sentences completed, words learned, XP and your daily streak
- Which kinds of mistakes you struggle with, so the coach screen can
  speak from real data — **only the type of mistake is stored, never the
  sentence you wrote**
- Word sets completed and your reading/listening progress
- Your theme and level preferences, your reminder time and your answer
  about cloud speech recognition

This data is stored **only on your device**; the app does not send it
anywhere. So that you do not lose your progress when you change phones,
this progress record is included in **your phone's own backup**: on
Android, the Android backup in your Google account and device-to-device
transfer; on iPhone, iCloud backup. That backup is kept by your phone's
system in your account; we cannot see or receive it. You can turn backup
off in your phone's settings.

**Deleting:** the **"Delete all my data"** option under Settings → My data
(shown in Turkish as "Bütün verilerimi sil" under "Verilerim") deletes all
of this data and the technical log from the device after you confirm;
this cannot be undone. Deleting the app also deletes the record on the
device. In both cases, a **backup your phone made earlier** is kept
separately: it may still contain the old record until your phone backs
up again, and it may be restored if you reinstall the app. You can delete
that backup in your phone's or your Google / Apple account's settings.

The **word database** (2,834 words: 2,815 words from the NGSL list and 19 day and month names) ships inside the app and works
entirely offline.

## Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

In Sentence Builder you can say a sentence you built out loud and have it
checked. This feature is **entirely optional**; if you never use it, the
microphone permission is never requested.

- The microphone opens **only when you tap the "Say" button on the "Say it
  out loud" card**, and it closes when you finish speaking.
- Audio is **not recorded or stored.** The app never writes the audio to a
  file; it only compares the **text** produced by your phone's speech
  recognition with the target sentence, then discards that text.
- Recognition is done by **your phone's own system**. If your phone runs
  Android 13 or later and supports on-device (offline) recognition, the
  app uses it and audio does not leave the phone.
- In every other case the app **asks you first.** These cases are: Android
  versions older than 13 (on these versions the speech engine does not
  guarantee offline processing), phones without on-device recognition,
  phones where the English recognition pack is not installed, and iPhone
  (the app cannot confirm that English speech will be recognized on the
  device on iPhone). The app's interface is Turkish; these are English
  translations of the dialogs.
  - On Android: "It can't be confirmed that your phone will recognize your
    speech on the device. For this feature, what you say may be sent to
    Google's service; the app doesn't store it. Continue?"
  - On iPhone: "On iPhone, it can't be guaranteed that your speech will be
    recognized on the device. For this feature, what you say may be sent to
    Apple's service; the app doesn't store it. Continue?"
  - **If you choose "Continue"**, the audio is processed by your phone's
    system service (on Android usually Google's speech service, on iOS
    Apple's) **under its own privacy policy**. That processing does not
    reach us; we never see or receive the audio.
  - **If you choose "No"**, audio does not leave the phone and the feature
    stays off.
  - Your answer is saved and you are not asked again. You can change it at
    any time with the **"Allow cloud speech recognition"** switch on the
    Settings screen (shown in Turkish as "Bulut ses tanımaya izin ver").
- You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's Settings; the
  rest of the app works exactly the same.

## Internet connection

We do not run a server of our own. The games, lessons and all progress
work without an internet connection, and the app does not send data over
the network on its own. The internet may come into play only in these
cases:

- If **you tap** the privacy-policy link in Settings or a site listed on
  the Sources screen, your phone's browser opens. Visiting that site is
  governed by the browser and by that site's own rules.
- In the "Say it out loud" feature, when the app cannot confirm that your
  voice will stay on the phone and **you have agreed**, the system's speech recognition
  service (section above).
- English text that has no recorded audio is read aloud by your phone's
  own text-to-speech engine. The app does not pick a network-based voice;
  it uses the engine's default English voice. If that voice has not been
  downloaded on your phone, the engine may, depending on its own settings,
  synthesize the text on its maker's server (for example Google's). In
  that case only the English text being read is sent, not your voice or
  any personal information.

This version has no Google Play leaderboard or any other online ranking.
If such a feature is added later, this policy will be updated before that
version is released.

---

## Reminder notifications

The app can send a daily practice reminder. These notifications are
created and delivered **entirely on your device**; they are not sent
from a server and no record is kept for them. You can turn them off in
Settings.

---

## Purchases and ads

This version contains **no in-app purchases and no ads**. The app does
not connect to the store's billing system, never asks for payment or
card details, and no content is locked.

---

## Children

CümleKur is intended for users **aged 13 and over** and is not directed
at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from
children — we do not collect personal information from anyone.

---

## Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is no record for you to ask
us to delete, correct or export. You can delete all data on your device
at any time with Settings → My data → **"Delete all my data"** (you are
asked to confirm; it cannot be undone). You can delete the copy in your
phone's backup in your phone's or your Google / Apple account's settings
(see "Deleting" above).

---

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. If a change ever
widens how data is used (for example, if an online feature is added), the
