CümleKur — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

CümleKur is an English-learning app made for Turkish speakers. This document states plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Short answer: There is no account, we never ask who you are, there are no ads and no tracking. All of your learning progress stays on your own device. The app does not send data to the internet on its own. The one thing to know about is the optional "Say it" feature: if your device has no on-device speech recognition, the app asks you first and the phone's system service processes the audio only if you agree (see the "Microphone" section below).

What we never collect

The app does not request, collect or transmit:

  • Name, e-mail address or phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos or files
  • Camera. The microphone is used only when you tap the "Say it" button; audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to us (separate section below)
  • Advertising identifier (the app contains no ads)
  • Usage analytics, or crash reports sent to us (the technical log described below never leaves your device)

We have no account system. You do not sign in to the app.

The technical log that stays on your device

If CümleKur closes unexpectedly, a short technical log of what happened is kept on your phone only. It is never sent anywhere and never reaches any server.

  • You can read it yourself under Settings → Technical Log.
  • You can delete it there with a single tap.
  • E-mail addresses, the user name inside file paths and long digit sequences are stripped out as it is written.
  • At most 10 entries are kept; the oldest is dropped when a new one arrives.

There is no automatic way to send it to us. If you want to, you can copy the text and send it yourself — the choice is entirely yours.

This is why the "we never collect" list above stays accurate: the log is not collected, it simply sits on your device.

What is stored on your device

The following is kept only on your own device:

  • Sentences completed, words learned, XP and your daily streak
  • Which kinds of mistakes you struggle with, so the coach screen can speak from real data — only the type of mistake is stored, never the sentence you wrote
  • Word sets completed and your reading/listening progress
  • Your theme, voice and level preferences

This data is stored only on your device; the app does not send it anywhere. So that you do not lose your progress when you change phones, this progress record is included in your phone's own backup: on Android, the Android backup in your Google account and device-to-device transfer; on iPhone, iCloud backup. That backup is kept by your phone's system in your account; we cannot see or receive it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings. Deleting the app deletes the record on the device.

The word database (2,818 words) ships inside the app and works entirely offline.

Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

In Sentence Builder you can say a sentence you built out loud and have it checked. This feature is entirely optional; if you never use it, the microphone permission is never requested.

  • The microphone opens only while you press the "Say it" button and closes when you stop speaking.
  • Audio is not recorded or stored. The app never writes the audio to a file; it only compares the text produced by your phone's speech recognition with the target sentence, then discards that text.
  • Recognition is done by your phone's own system. If your device supports on-device recognition, the app uses it and audio does not leave the phone.
  • If your device does not support on-device recognition (or the English recognition pack is not installed), the app asks you first: "Your phone can't recognize speech on the device. For this feature, your voice will be sent to Google's service; the app doesn't store it. Continue?" (on iPhone: "Apple's service"). The app's interface is Turkish; this is the English translation of the dialog.
  • If you choose "Continue", the audio is processed by your phone's system service (on Android usually Google's speech service, on iOS Apple's) under its own privacy policy. That processing does not reach us; we never see or receive the audio.
  • If you choose "No", audio does not leave the phone and the feature stays off.
  • Your answer is saved and you are not asked again. You can change it at any time with the "Allow cloud speech recognition" switch on the Settings screen (shown in Turkish as "Bulut ses tanımaya izin ver").
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's Settings; the rest of the app works exactly the same.

Internet connection

We do not run a server of our own. The games, lessons and all progress work without an internet connection, and the app does not send data over the network on its own. The internet comes into play only in two cases:

  • If you tap the privacy-policy link in Settings or a site listed on the Sources screen, your phone's browser opens. Visiting that site is governed by the browser and by that site's own rules.
  • In the "Say it" feature, when the device has no on-device recognition and you have agreed, the system's speech recognition service (section above).

This version has no Google Play leaderboard or any other online ranking. If such a feature is added later, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Reminder notifications

The app can send a daily practice reminder. These notifications are created and delivered entirely on your device; they are not sent from a server and no record is kept for them. You can turn them off in Settings.

Purchases and ads

This version contains no in-app purchases and no ads. The app does not connect to the store's billing system, never asks for payment or card details, and no content is locked.

Children

CümleKur is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to delete, correct or export. You can remove all on-device progress at any time by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. If a change ever widens how data is used (for example, if an online feature is added), the policy will be updated before that version is released.
