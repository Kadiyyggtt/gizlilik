AkademikBulucu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This policy applies to the AkademikBulucu application.

In short

You never need an account. Nothing identifying you is ever requested.

The app searches academic papers, so the search terms you type do go to the internet — otherwise no search could happen. Those queries are not stored, are not linked to your identity, and are never used for advertising.

The papers you save, your folders and your notes stay on your phone only.

What is sent

When you search, only the words you typed are sent.

The path is:

`` Your phone → AkademikBulucu server → academic databases ``

The AkademikBulucu server (running on Cloudflare Workers) receives your query and forwards it to these public academic sources:

  • Crossref — the publishers' shared record of published works
  • arXiv — open-access preprint archive
  • Semantic Scholar — academic search service

The results are merged and returned to you.

Not sent with that request: your name, e-mail address, phone number, device identifier, location or advertising identifier. None of these are ever requested by the app in the first place.

What is not stored

Our server does not keep your search queries. The query is processed, the result is returned, and no record is written. There are no user accounts, no user identifiers and no session tracking; linking a search to a particular person is not technically possible for us.

The only thing stored on the server is the daily academic digest — the same content for everyone, not personalised. It is deleted automatically after three days.

AI summaries

When you ask for a plain-language summary of a paper, that paper's title and abstract are sent to Google Gemini.

  • What is sent is publicly available paper information that came from the academic databases; it is not text belonging to you.
  • No personal information of yours is added to the request.
  • This feature runs only when you ask for it.

What stays on your phone

The following are stored on your device only and are never sent anywhere:

  • The papers and folders you save
  • The notes you write on papers
  • Your search history
  • Your theme preference (light/dark)
  • Your vibration preference

They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

What we never collect

  • Name, e-mail address, phone number
  • Account or sign-in details
  • Location
  • Contacts or photos
  • Advertising identifier
  • Usage analytics, or crash reports sent to us (the technical log described below never leaves your device)

The technical log that stays on your device

If AkademikBulucu closes unexpectedly, a short technical log of what happened is kept on your phone only. It is never sent anywhere and never reaches any server.

  • You can read it yourself under Settings → Technical Log.
  • You can delete it there with a single tap.
  • E-mail addresses, the user name inside file paths and long digit sequences are stripped out as it is written.
  • At most 10 entries are kept; the oldest is dropped when a new one arrives.

There is no automatic way to send it to us. If you want to, you can copy the text and send it yourself — the choice is entirely yours.

This is why the "we never collect" list above stays accurate: the log is not collected, it simply sits on your device.

Advertising and purchases

  • There are no ads. No advertising network or tracking tool is used.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

External links

When you tap a paper to read it, the publisher's site or the PDF link opens in your phone's browser. Those sites are not ours and their own privacy policies apply.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only. It does not request location, microphone, camera, contacts, photo or storage permissions.

When you export a bibliography, your phone's own share sheet opens; the file goes where you choose and is not sent to us.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for academic research and is not directed at children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, none is collected from children either.
