# Legion Run — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

**In short: Legion Run collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.**

- **No account.** The game never asks for a name, e-mail or phone number.
- **No internet.** The app does not request the internet permission and connects to no server.
- **No ads, no purchases.** This version contains no advertising and no real-money purchases.
- **No analytics or tracking.** No usage statistics, advertising ID or location are collected.
- **Progress stays on your phone.** Your level, gold, upgrades and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only in the app's folder on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Permissions:** only *vibration* (for in-game feedback; can be turned off in Settings).
- **Children:** the game is bloodless and, collecting no personal data, collects none from children.
