# FAMA — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 12 September 2026

FAMA is a text-based fame simulation game. This document states plainly
what the game touches and what it does not.

**Short answer: no information is collected.** There is no account, no
advertising, no internet requirement and not a single permission
request. The game runs entirely on your device.

---

## What we never collect

The game does not request, collect or transmit:

- Name, e-mail address, phone number or date of birth
- Location
- Contacts, calendar, photos or files
- Camera or microphone
- **Advertising identifier** — the game contains no ads
- Usage analytics, or crash reports **sent to us**
  (the technical log described below never leaves your device)

The game requests no permissions. There is **no account system.**

---

## The technical log that stays on your device

If FAMA closes unexpectedly, a short technical log of what happened is kept
**on your phone only**. It is never sent anywhere and never reaches any
server.

- You can read it yourself under **Settings → Technical log**.
- You can delete it there with a single tap.
- E-mail addresses, the user name inside file paths and long digit
  sequences are stripped out as it is written.
- At most 10 entries are kept; the oldest is dropped when a new one arrives.

There is no automatic way to send it to us. If you want to, you can copy
the text and send it yourself — the choice is entirely yours.

This is why the "we never collect" list above stays accurate: the log is
**not collected**, it simply sits on your device.

## Internet

**The game works offline.** It connects to no server and sends no
requests. It is fully playable in airplane mode.

---

## What is stored on your device

Only the following is kept **on your own device**:

- The history of your runs and the permanent traits you have unlocked
- Your sound and language preferences

None of this leaves the device. Deleting the game deletes all of it.

---

## Sharing

When a run ends you can share the **Life Card**. That image is the
game's own drawing; no camera or photo library is used. Tapping share
opens your phone's **own share sheet** and you choose where the image
goes. The image is never sent to us and we cannot see it.

What happens to the image afterwards is governed by the privacy terms of
the app you chose.

---

## Age rating

FAMA is intended for ages 12 and over. The content contains no drugs,
crime, sexual content or graphic violence; the stories deal with fame,
money, rivalry, family and friendship. It is not directed at children,
and in any case it collects no information from anyone.

---

## If this ever changes

If advertising or in-app purchases are ever added, this policy will be
updated **beforehand** and the change announced inside the app. As of
today the game contains neither.

---

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to delete,
correct or export. You can remove all on-device progress by deleting the
game.
