FAMA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

FAMA is a text-based fame simulation game. This document states plainly what the game touches and what it does not.

Short answer: no information is collected. There is no account, no advertising, no internet requirement and not a single permission request. The game runs entirely on your device.

What we never collect

The game does not request, collect or transmit:

  • Name, e-mail address, phone number or date of birth
  • Location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos or files
  • Camera or microphone
  • Advertising identifier — the game contains no ads
  • Usage analytics or crash reporting

The game requests no permissions. There is no account system.

Internet

The game works offline. It connects to no server and sends no requests. It is fully playable in airplane mode.

What is stored on your device

Only the following is kept on your own device:

  • The history of your runs and the permanent traits you have unlocked
  • Your sound and language preferences

None of this leaves the device. Deleting the game deletes all of it.

Sharing

When a run ends you can share the Life Card. That image is the game's own drawing; no camera or photo library is used. Tapping share opens your phone's own share sheet and you choose where the image goes. The image is never sent to us and we cannot see it.

What happens to the image afterwards is governed by the privacy terms of the app you chose.

Age rating

FAMA is intended for ages 12 and over. The content contains no drugs, crime, sexual content or graphic violence; the stories deal with fame, money, rivalry, family and friendship. It is not directed at children, and in any case it collects no information from anyone.

If this ever changes

If advertising or in-app purchases are ever added, this policy will be updated beforehand and the change announced inside the app. As of today the game contains neither.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to delete, correct or export. You can remove all on-device progress by deleting the game.
