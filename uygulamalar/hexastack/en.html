Hexa Stack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: Hexa Stack has no server of its own and never asks for personal information. The game plays offline. The one exception is the optional Google Play Games service: if you are signed in to Play Games, the highest level you have beaten is sent to Google for the leaderboard and the cloud save.

  • No account. The game never asks for a name, e-mail or phone number.
  • The game itself does not use the internet. The app sends no data to any server on its own; progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • Google Play Games leaderboard (optional). When the game shows a "Leaderboard" button it uses Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Play Games, Google processes your Play Games player identity (gamer name and avatar) and the highest level you have beaten in this game (your score). According to Google's Play Games documentation the SDK also collects analytics and diagnostics data to improve the service, and data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Who can see your entry is set by the visibility setting in your Play Games profile. This data is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — you can delete Play Games data at https://play.google.com/games/profile and https://myaccount.google.com. If you do not sign in, no score is sent and the game works the same.
  • Google Play Games cloud save (optional). If you are signed in to Play Games, the number of the level you have reached is stored in Google Play Games' saved games service, linked to your Google account, so you can continue where you left off after changing phones or reinstalling. The save contains only the level number, no name, e-mail or anything else. You can delete it from the Play Games app or at https://myaccount.google.com.
  • Rating card. After a few levels, Google Play's own "Rate this game" card may appear. Google shows the card; your rating and review never reach the game and go straight to Google Play.
  • No ads, no purchases. This version contains no advertising and no real-money purchases.
  • No analytics or tracking. The game itself collects no usage statistics, advertising ID or location.
  • Progress on your phone. Your level and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored in the app's folder on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. If you are signed in to Play Games, a copy of the level number alone is also kept in the cloud save described above.
  • Permissions: only vibration (a short buzz when a stack clears and when a level is complete; can be turned off in Settings). For the leaderboard and the cloud save, Google Play services makes the connection.
  • Privacy link: the "Privacy" row in Settings opens this page in your browser (https://kadiyyggtt.github.io/gizlilik/uygulamalar/hexastack/en.html); the game itself never goes online.
  • More games: this row in Settings opens the developer's page on Google Play. The game sends no information when you tap it.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: yyggttkadir@gmail.com
