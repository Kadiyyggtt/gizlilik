# Tiny Isles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

**In short: Tiny Isles collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.**

- **No account.** The game never asks for a name, e-mail or phone number.
- **No internet.** The app does not request the internet permission and connects to no server.
- **No ads, no purchases.** This version contains no advertising and no real-money purchases. In-game gold is earned only by playing and is only used to open land and buy upgrades inside the game.
- **No analytics or tracking.** No usage statistics, advertising ID or location are collected.
- **Progress stays on your phone.** Your gold, the items in your backpack, the land you opened, the upgrades and workers you bought, when trees grow back, the moment you last left the game (to work out how long your workers kept going) and your settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only in the app's folder on your device, in an encrypted file. This file is not included in Android backup either. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Permissions:** only vibration (for in-game feedback; can be turned off in Settings).
- **Children:** there is no violence, no chat and no contact with other players; collecting no personal data, the game collects none from children.
- **Privacy link:** the "Privacy" row in Settings opens this page in your browser; the game itself never goes online.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: yyggttkadir@gmail.com
