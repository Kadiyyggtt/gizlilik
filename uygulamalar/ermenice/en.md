# Ermenice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

**Ermenice does not ask you to create an account and shows no ads. The app has no analytics tool that tracks how you use it. Your learning data is stored on your device and is not sent to the developer. Subscription data is processed by RevenueCat for subscription management and aggregate sales statistics; payment is taken by the app store. Even if you do not subscribe, the app connects to RevenueCat each time it starts to check the subscription status.**

This text also serves as the information notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

## Data controller
Arzu Yiğit, individual developer. Email: yyggttkadir@gmail.com

## What is stored on your device
Your name, your progress (XP, streak, completed lessons, badges), the items you got wrong and your settings are stored on this device and are not sent to the developer or to any server. You can reset them at any time from the Settings screen.

If your phone's backup setting is on, this information may be backed up together with your phone's own backup: to your Google account on Android or, on Apple devices, to iCloud or to a device backup on your computer. Google or Apple keeps these backups under its own privacy policy; the developer cannot access them. You can turn backups on or off in your phone's settings. When you change phones or reinstall the app, your progress may be restored from this backup.

If you delete the app, the information on the device is deleted as well; a phone backup taken earlier may remain in your account for some time, depending on your phone's backup settings.

## Subscription and payment
The Premium subscription is purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store. These stores process the payment under their own privacy policies; the developer never sees your card details.

To verify the subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as its subscription infrastructure. Each time the app starts, whether or not you subscribe, it connects to RevenueCat to check the subscription status. It also connects when you open the payment screen, to get current prices, and when you make or restore a purchase. During these connections, the following is sent to RevenueCat:
- a random anonymous user ID generated by the app (not your name, email or phone number),
- subscription status and, if you have made a purchase, the transaction details provided by the store (product, date, transaction number, price and currency),
- device and system information (device model, operating system version, app version, language and country settings) and, during the connection, your IP address.

This information is not matched with your identity (name, email), is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold.

## Purpose and legal basis
Subscription data is processed to unlock Premium content, verify the subscription, restore purchases and answer your subscription-related requests. The legal basis for this is KVKK Article 5(2)(c): it is necessary for the conclusion and performance of the subscription contract.

For users who do not subscribe, the subscription check made each time the app starts processes the anonymous user ID, device and system information and the IP address. The purpose is to determine whether Premium content is unlocked and to show current prices on the payment screen. Since there is no subscription contract yet, the legal basis for this is KVKK Article 5(2)(f): provided that it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms, the processing is necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller. Here, the legitimate interest is correctly separating free and Premium content and being able to offer the subscription.

Subscription data is also processed in the RevenueCat dashboard for aggregate sales statistics (for example, the number of subscribers and the renewal and cancellation rates). These statistics show totals, not individual people, and do not include how you use the app. The legal basis for this is KVKK Article 5(2)(f): provided that it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms, the processing is necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller. Here, the legitimate interest is maintaining the service.

## How it is collected
Subscription data is collected automatically and electronically through the RevenueCat software in the app when the app checks the subscription status each time it starts, when you open the payment screen and when you make or restore a purchase.

## Transfers, including transfers abroad
Subscription data is transferred to RevenueCat as a service provider for the purposes above. RevenueCat's servers are outside Türkiye (USA); this is therefore a transfer abroad within the meaning of KVKK Article 9. Your payment details are processed directly by Google or Apple. The data is not shared with any other third party.

## Retention
Subscription data is kept for as long as the subscription relationship continues and for the period required by legal obligations; it is then deleted or anonymized.

Whether or not you subscribe, the anonymous record created or updated each time the app starts (anonymous user ID, device and system information and, if you have made a purchase, the transaction details) is kept by RevenueCat on behalf of the developer. Subject to any legal retention obligations, this record is deleted no later than 1 year after the subscription relationship ends (if you have not subscribed, 1 year after you last opened the app); on request, it is deleted immediately, to the extent the record can be identified. The developer deletes the record through RevenueCat; according to RevenueCat, this deletion also covers the purchase history linked to the record. Because the anonymous ID is not matched with your name or email, finding the record depends on the order or transaction number given by the store for a subscription; if you have not made a purchase, it may not be possible to identify the record as yours.

## Internet connection
Lessons work offline. The app connects to the internet only for the subscription: to check the subscription status each time it starts, to show current prices on the payment screen, and to make or restore purchases. Without an internet connection this check cannot be made, but lessons keep working.

## Notifications
If you turn on the daily reminder, your device shows a local notification at the time you choose. It does not come from the internet and is not connected to any server. You can turn it off from the Settings screen at any time.

## Microphone
When a lesson introduces a new letter, word or sentence, a microphone button appears. If you press it, you can record your own voice and compare it with the voice in the app. Microphone permission is requested only for this button. The recording is kept temporarily on your phone only for the comparison; it is deleted when you make a new recording, move to another item or leave that screen, and it is not included in phone backups. If the app closes unexpectedly, any remaining recording is deleted the next time the app starts. The recording is not sent anywhere or shared with anyone, and no speech recognition is performed. If you do not grant the permission, the rest of the app works as usual.

## Backup code
The backup code in Settings is a text version of the name you entered and your progress. It is shared only if you share it yourself; the app does not send it anywhere.

## Audio files
The Armenian audio was generated with artificial intelligence. It comes bundled inside the app and plays without an internet connection.

## Ads and tracking
There are no ads and no tracking. The app has no analytics tool that tracks how you use it. Subscription data is processed by RevenueCat for subscription management and aggregate sales statistics. You are not asked to create an account.

## Your rights under KVKK
Under KVKK Article 11 you may apply to the data controller to:
- learn whether your personal data is processed,
- request information if it is,
- learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
- know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
- request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
- request deletion or destruction under the conditions of KVKK Article 7,
- request that third parties to whom the data was transferred be notified of such correction, deletion or destruction,
- object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis of the data,
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

## How to apply
You can send your requests by email to yyggttkadir@gmail.com. State your name, contact details and request clearly; for subscription-related requests, adding the order or transaction number from the store makes it easier to find the record. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest and, as a rule, free of charge. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

## Children
The app asks for no personal information other than a name; the name is not sent to the developer either and exists only on your device and, if backups are on, in your phone's own backup. Subscriptions are purchased through the store account and the store's family settings; users under 18 must get permission from a parent or guardian before purchasing.
