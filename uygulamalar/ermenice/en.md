# Ermenice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

**Ermenice is free and does not ask you to create an account. The app does not connect to the internet. No data is collected; your learning data is stored on your device and is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.**

The developer does not access or process any personal data through the app. This text has been prepared to inform you under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

## Data controller
Arzu Yiğit, individual developer. Email: yyggttkadir@gmail.com

## What is stored on your device
Your name, your progress (XP, streak, completed lessons, badges), the items you got wrong and your settings are stored on this device and are not sent to the developer or to any server. You can reset them at any time from the Settings screen.

If your phone's backup setting is on, this information may also be included in your phone's own backup: to your Google account on Android or, on Apple devices, to iCloud or to a device backup on your computer. Google or Apple keeps these backups under its own privacy policy; the developer cannot access them. You can turn backups on or off in your phone's settings. When you change phones or reinstall the app, your progress may be restored from this backup.

If you delete the app, the information on the device is deleted as well; a phone backup taken earlier may remain in your account or on your computer for some time, depending on your phone's backup settings.

## Internet connection
The app does not connect to the internet. Lessons, audio and all other content come bundled inside the app and work offline. The app has no analytics tool that tracks how you use it and no tool that sends error reports.

## Notifications
If you turn on the daily reminder, your device shows a local notification at the time you choose. It does not come from the internet and is not connected to any server. You can turn it off from the Settings screen at any time.

## Microphone
When a lesson introduces a new letter, word or sentence, a microphone button appears. If you press it, you can record your own voice and compare it with the voice in the app. Microphone permission is requested only for this button. The recording is kept temporarily on your phone only for the comparison; it is deleted when you make a new recording, move to another item or leave that screen, and it is not included in phone backups. If the app closes unexpectedly, any remaining recording is deleted the next time the app starts. The recording is not sent anywhere or shared with anyone, and no speech recognition is performed. If you do not grant the permission, the rest of the app works as usual.

## Backup code
The backup code in Settings is a text version of the name you entered and your progress. It reaches others only if you share it yourself; the app does not send it anywhere.

## Audio files
The Armenian audio was generated with artificial intelligence. It comes bundled inside the app and plays without an internet connection.

## Your rights under KVKK
Under KVKK Article 11 you may apply to the data controller to exercise your rights, such as learning whether your personal data is processed, requesting information if it is, and requesting its correction or deletion. Because the app does not collect any data, the developer holds no record about you. You can delete the information on your device yourself by resetting it from the Settings screen or by deleting the app.

## How to apply
You can send your requests by email to yyggttkadir@gmail.com. State your name, contact details and request clearly. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest and, as a rule, free of charge. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

## Children
The app asks for no personal information other than a name. The name is not sent to the developer either; it exists only on your device and, if backups are on, in your phone's own backup.

## Changes
Changes to this policy will be announced in the new version of the app and on this page. If a feature that processes data is added to the app in the future, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
