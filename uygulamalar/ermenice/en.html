Ermenice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The Ermenice app collects no personal data from you, sends nothing to a server and does not ask you to create an account.

What stays on your device

Your name, your progress (XP, streak, finished lessons, badges), the items you got wrong and your settings are stored only on this device. If you delete the app, they are deleted too. You can also reset them from the Settings screen.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, your device shows a local notification at the time you choose. It does not come from the internet and is not connected to any server. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Microphone

When a lesson introduces a new letter, word or sentence, a microphone button appears. If you press it, you can record your own voice and compare it with the voice in the app. Microphone permission is requested only for this button. The recording stays in a temporary file on your phone, is not sent anywhere, your speech is not recognized and it is not shared with anyone else. If you do not grant permission, the rest of the app works exactly the same.

Backup code

The backup code in Settings is a text version of your progress. You are the one who shares it; the app does not send it to any server.

Audio files

The Armenian audio comes bundled inside the app and plays without an internet connection. The audio was generated with artificial intelligence.

Ads and tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics tools, no tracking and no third-party accounts.

Children

Because the app asks for no personal information, it is suitable for users of every age.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be announced in the new version of the app and on this page.
