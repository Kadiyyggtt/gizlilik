Ermenice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Ermenice does not ask you to create an account and shows no ads. The app has no analytics tool that tracks how you use it. Your learning data is stored on your device and is not sent to the developer. Subscription data is processed by RevenueCat for subscription management and aggregate sales statistics; payment is taken by the app store.

This text also serves as the information notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Data controller

[Data controller full name], individual developer. E-mail: yyggttkadir@gmail.com

What is stored on your device

Your name, your progress (XP, streak, finished lessons, badges), the items you got wrong and your settings are stored on this device and are not sent to the developer or to any server. You can reset them at any time from the Settings screen.

If your phone's backup setting is on, this information may be backed up together with your phone's own backup: to your Google account on Android, and to iCloud or to a device backup on your computer on Apple devices. Google or Apple keeps these backups under their own privacy policies; the developer cannot access them. You can turn backups on or off in your phone's settings. When you change phones or reinstall the app, your progress may be restored from this backup.

If you delete the app, the information on the device is deleted as well; a phone backup taken earlier may remain in your account for some time, depending on your phone's backup settings.

Subscription and payment

The Premium subscription is purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store. These stores process the payment under their own privacy policies; the developer never sees your card details.

To verify the subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as its subscription service. When you make or restore a purchase, or when the app checks the subscription status, the following is sent to RevenueCat:

  • a random anonymous user ID generated in the app (not your name, e-mail or phone number),
  • purchase and subscription status and the transaction details provided by the store (product, date, transaction number, price and currency),
  • device and system information (device model, operating system version, app version, language and region setting) and, during the connection, your IP address.

This information is not matched with your identity (name, e-mail), is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold.

Purpose and legal basis

Subscription data is processed to unlock Premium content, verify the subscription, restore purchases and answer your subscription-related requests. The legal basis for this is KVKK Article 5(2)(c): it is necessary for the conclusion and performance of the subscription contract.

Subscription data is also processed in the RevenueCat dashboard for aggregate sales statistics (for example, the number of subscribers and the renewal and cancellation rates). These statistics show totals, not individual people, and do not include how you use the app. The legal basis for this is KVKK Article 5(2)(f): the processing is necessary for the data controller's legitimate interest in running the service.

How it is collected

Subscription data is collected automatically and electronically through the RevenueCat software in the app when you make or restore a purchase and when the app checks the subscription status.

Transfers, including transfers abroad

Subscription data is transferred to RevenueCat as a service provider for the purposes above. RevenueCat's servers are outside Türkiye (USA); this is therefore a transfer abroad within the meaning of KVKK Article 9. Your payment details are processed directly by Google or Apple. The data is not shared with any other third party.

Retention

Subscription data is kept for as long as the subscription relationship continues and for the period required by legal obligations; it is then deleted or anonymized.

Internet connection

Lessons work offline. The app connects to the internet only for purchases, restoring purchases and subscription checks.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, your device shows a local notification at the time you choose. It does not come from the internet and is not connected to any server. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Microphone

When a lesson introduces a new letter, word or sentence, a microphone button appears. If you press it, you can record your own voice and compare it with the voice in the app. Microphone permission is requested only for this button. The recording is kept temporarily on your phone only for the comparison; it is deleted when you make a new recording, move to another item or leave that screen, and it is not included in phone backups. If the app closes unexpectedly, any remaining recording is deleted the next time the app starts. It is not sent anywhere, no speech recognition is performed and it is not shared with anyone. If you do not grant the permission, the rest of the app works as usual.

Backup code

The backup code in Settings is a text version of the name you entered and your progress. It is shared only if you share it yourself; the app does not send it anywhere.

Audio files

The Armenian audio was generated with artificial intelligence. It comes bundled inside the app and plays without an internet connection.

Ads and tracking

There are no ads and no tracking. The app has no analytics tool that tracks how you use it. Subscription data is processed by RevenueCat for subscription management and aggregate sales statistics. You are not asked to create an account.

Your rights under KVKK

Under KVKK Article 11 you may apply to the data controller to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • request information if it is,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
  • request deletion or destruction under the conditions of KVKK Article 7,
  • request that third parties to whom the data was transferred be notified of such correction, deletion or destruction,
  • object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis of the data,
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to apply

Send your request to yyggttkadir@gmail.com by e-mail or in writing. State your name, contact details and request clearly; for subscription-related requests, adding the order or transaction number from the store makes it easier to find the record. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest and, as a rule, free of charge. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

Children

The app asks for no personal information other than a name; the name is not sent to the developer either and exists only on your device and, if backups are on, in your phone's own backup. Subscriptions are purchased through the store account and the store's family settings; users under 18 must get permission from a parent or guardian before purchasing.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be announced in the new version of the app and on this page.

Contact

Questions: yyggttkadir@gmail.com
