Penalty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Penalty is a football game. This document states plainly what the game touches and what it does not.

Short answer: no ads, no purchases, no account, no server of ours. The game collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It works fully offline.

What we do not collect

The game does not ask for or collect:

  • Name, e-mail, phone number, date of birth
  • Location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files
  • Camera or microphone
  • Advertising ID or any other device identifier
  • Usage statistics, analytics tools, crash reports sent to us

What stays on your device

The game stores only the following on your phone; none of it is sent anywhere:

  • Your settings (sound, vibration, language)
  • Your cup progress (chosen team, stages won, match counts)
  • Technical log: if the game ever closes unexpectedly, a short record of the error. You can view and delete it under Settings → Technical log. We cannot see it.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of it. Settings → "Reset cup progress" clears your progress from inside the game.

Permissions

The game requests no special permissions. Vibration uses Android's standard VIBRATE permission and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone and collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with its date.
