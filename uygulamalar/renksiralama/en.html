Color Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Color Sort is a puzzle game. This document states plainly which information the game touches and which it does not.

Short answer: in this version there are no ads, no purchases, no account and no server of ours. The game collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It works fully offline.

What we never collect

The game does not request or collect:

  • Name, e-mail, phone number, date of birth
  • Location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files
  • Camera and microphone
  • Advertising ID or any other device identifier
  • Usage statistics, analytics tools, crash reports sent to us

There is no account system. There is no server of ours. There is no third-party advertising or analytics software in the game.

Permissions

You are not asked for any permission. Location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos and storage are not requested. On iOS no "tracking" prompt appears.

Technical permissions that may show on the store page are ones Android grants at install time without a prompt:

PermissionWhy
VibrationA short buzz when a ball lands or a tube is completed — can be turned off in Settings
Audio settingsSo game sounds don't stop your own music
InternetFramework default; the game does not connect

The advertising ID permission (Android) is explicitly blocked.

Ads and purchases

This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. The whole game, including every helper (undo, hint, extra tube), is free.

If ads are added in a future update, this policy will be changed before that update, listing exactly what is shared.

Internet

The game works offline: levels are generated on your phone and your progress is stored on your phone. The game connects to no server.

What is stored on your device

Only on your own device:

  • Which level you are on and the highest level completed
  • An unfinished level (so you can continue where you left off)
  • Ball styles and themes you unlocked and selected
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, language, colour-blind mode)
  • Technical log: if the game closes unexpectedly, a short error record (at most 10). It stays on the phone and is never sent to us; you can view and delete it in Settings → Technical log.

None of this is sent anywhere. Settings → Reset game clears your progress; deleting the game removes everything.

Children

Color Sort is not offered as an app directed at children. With no account, no data collection and no ads it carries no personal-data risk.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete, correct or export. You can reset progress in the game or delete the game to remove everything.
