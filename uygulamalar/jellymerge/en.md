# Jelly Merge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

**In short: Jelly Merge collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.**

- **No account.** The game never asks for a name, e-mail or phone number.
- **No internet.** The app does not request internet access and connects to no server.
- **No ads, no purchases.** This version has no advertising and no real-money sales.
- **No analytics, no tracking.** No usage statistics, advertising ID or location are collected.
- **Saved only on your phone.** Your best score, an unfinished game (the jellies in the box and the score) and your settings (sound, vibration, language) are kept only in the app's folder on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Permissions:** only **vibration** (a short buzz when jellies merge; can be turned off in Settings).
- **Privacy link:** the "Privacy" row in Settings opens this page in your browser; the game itself never goes online.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: yyggttkadir@gmail.com
