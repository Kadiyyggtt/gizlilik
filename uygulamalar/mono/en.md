# MONO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

**In short: MONO collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.**

- **No account.** The app never asks for a name, e-mail or phone number.
- **No internet.** The app does not request the internet permission and connects to no server.
- **No ads, no purchases.** This version contains no advertising and no real-money purchases.
- **No analytics or tracking.** No usage statistics, advertising ID or location are collected.
- **Your progress stays on your phone.** Each game's best score, play counts and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are stored only in the app's folder on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also erase your records yourself from the settings screen.
- **Permissions:** only **vibration** (for in-game feedback; can be turned off in Settings).
- **Children:** the games contain no violence and, since no personal data is collected, none is collected from children.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: yyggttkadir@gmail.com
