# Little Farm (Minik Çiftlik) — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 29 September 2026

Little Farm is an animal-care game for children aged 3–7. This page says
plainly what the game touches and what it does not.

**Short answer:** This version has no ads, no real-money purchases, no
accounts and no analytics. The game connects to no server and sends no
information off the device. It works the same without internet.

---

## What we do not collect

The game **does not ask for or collect:**

- Name, e-mail, phone number, date of birth
- Location
- Contacts, calendar, photos, files
- Camera and microphone
- An advertising ID or any other device identifier
- Usage statistics, crash reports, analytics

The game's code connects to no server; there is nowhere to send anything
to. In short, the game **collects no personal information**.

## What stays on the device

The game stores only **its own progress** on the device: which animals
are unlocked, their meters, the eggs and stars in the basket, accessories
bought in the market, whether the tutorial was shown, and the parents'
settings (sound, music, language, reminders, time-of-day sky). This
information does not identify anyone, never leaves the device, and can be
erased with **Parents area → Reset progress**. Uninstalling the app also
erases it.

Whether the sky shows day, evening or night, and which season it is, is
worked out from the device's own clock and date; that never leaves the
device either.

## Children

The game is made for children and asks them for no personal information.
Settings sit behind a simple addition question that a grown-up answers at
once (parental gate). There are no ads, external links, social sharing,
chat or links to other apps.

## Purchases

There are **no real-money purchases**. Animals and accessories in the
market are bought only with the game's own eggs and stars.

## Notifications

Now and then the game may show a reminder, such as "The chicken misses
you" or "Something is waiting in the market". These are **local
notifications**: they are set on the device and fire from the device.
There is no server sending them; no push token is requested and nothing is
sent to anyone.

- Never between 20:00 and 09:00; at most two a day.
- The notification permission is asked once, on the first "Play" tap.
  The "Reminders" switch in the parents' area turns them off completely;
  so do the device settings.

## Permissions

**The only permission asked for is notifications** (above). Location,
camera, microphone, contacts, photos and storage are never requested. The
store page may list the internet permission Android adds to every app;
the game does not use it.

## Third parties

**There are no third parties.** No ad network, analytics, crash reporter,
payment service or social media library is used. Sounds and pictures are
generated inside the app and are not downloaded.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes;
the store page is updated at the same time. If the game's behaviour is
ever going to differ from what is written here (for example a new
service), this text changes first.
